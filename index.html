00:01 — ABOUT

I'm a freelance videographer, gaffer, and editor working out of The Hague. Most of my jobs start the same way: walk the location, figure out where the light needs to go, and build the shot from there. Get that part right, and the camera work and the edit tend to fall into place.

Over the past ten years that's meant gaffing for other DPs, shooting and lighting my own jobs, and finishing other people's footage in the edit bay when a project needs an extra hand. I work alone on small shoots and slot into bigger crews when a job calls for it.
